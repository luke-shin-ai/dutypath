What this page computes, and what it does not

The rate on a date and the duty on a value are computed here, in your browser, from the schedule rows in the bundle: a base rate and a staging category for the United States and the European Union, and the printed yearly cells for Japan. Change the date and the numbers move.

The origin verdict is not computed here. It comes from the engine in this repository, which read the rule sentences and the exclusion intervals; the page shows its verdict, the rule code and the sentence it followed. tests/test_demo.py fails if the bundle and the engine ever disagree.

The rate arithmetic above is the only thing the page does twice. scripts/check_demo.py compares it with the engine's on seven dates for every schedule row in the bundle, so the two cannot drift apart quietly.

The page makes no network call and writes nothing. The claim window is the Korean one year post import period, quoted from the snapshot's own deadline row.
